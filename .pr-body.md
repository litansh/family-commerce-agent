Coverage stops being a reason to hide a store and becomes a fact printed on that store's card. The only store without a card now is a store that does not deliver to the address.

Moved and sized, never removed.

**Unfolded, the swaps.** `לחם אחיד → לחם זיתים 540 גרם  ₪24.90` and `שמן זית → שמן זית כתית מעולה 500 מל CIR…  ₪27.90`, each with `את זה שביקשתם יש בשופרסל ONLINE · רמי לוי אונליין` under it in amber. **The first thing the finished card found was a bad substitute.** Tiv Taam swaps **לחם אחיד → לחם זיתים** — olive bread for plain sliced bread. That would have turned up in the basket. Naming every swap is what surfaced it; the swap itself is api-fixer / product-qa ground (the "no pickles for cucumbers" rule does not catch bread). Recorded in the backlog.

## What is in the change

## The rule the module exists for

`storefrontLines[…].price` is the provider's **unit** price; a store's subtotal is built from `lineTotal`, which carries promotions. On the committed capture the two disagree by **₪8.41 at Rami Levy, ₪10.11 at Tiv Taam, ₪14.46 at Carrefour, ₪20.81 at Victory — and ₪0.00 at all four Wolt venues**, which is what points at promotions rather than at a bug. A card whose line prices do not add up to its own headline breaks promise 4 directly.

So `fullBasket.ts` takes every total from the engine — a single-leg option's own cash, a leg's subtotal, a rejected row's `itemsSubtotal` — and **where no engine number exists it returns `undefined` and the card shows none**. `e2e/full-basket.mjs` asserts the gap on every card, so the day someone tries to sum prices it goes red.

## What this hands to api-fixer, in the order the screen needs it

1. **`storefronts[sid].deliveryFee`** — the highest-value field, above the full/exact totals themselves. Today only stores that became options carry a fee, so eight of twelve cards read `+ משלוח` in muted ink and cannot sit in one price column. One field turns them all into delivered totals.
2. **`storefrontLines[…][…].lineTotal`** — what a line costs inside the basket, so the swap list stops falling back to a shelf price.
3. **`fullBasket` / `exactBasket` totals** — states 3, 4 and 12 light up; the second number gets its shekels.
4. **`cheaper[sid]`** — `עשה את זה זול יותר` is built and dark on purpose: the card shows the action only when the engine can answer it. An empty array is "looked, found nothing" (a sentence); an absent key is "nobody looked" (no button). **A button that does nothing is worse than no button**, and this way the action lights up with no screen change. Both branches are unit-tested already.

Coverage rejections are also still real for the Wolt venues (3–4 of 8 lines, no alternatives offered) — a lookup that did not run, not a store without eggs.
